M5 Whole Health: Terms of Use

Effective October 19, 2026 · Privacy policy · Open the app

M5 Whole Health ("M5") is a free, open-source web app that helps you gather, review and share your own health records. It is a demonstration project, available for anyone to use, published by Mark Roberts as an individual. It is not a supported production service: it may change, break or go offline without notice. By using M5 you agree to these terms and the privacy policy.

What M5 does

  • Imports health records you choose to bring in: from a health system you sign in to (for example through Epic MyChart), from a SMART Health Link, or from a file you upload.
  • Shows them in one place, points out duplicates and missing information, and can score data quality with the PIQI framework.
  • Compares insurance plans and lets you share selected information as a SMART Health Link or PDF.

Your data stays with you

M5 runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server and no account. The records you import are stored only in your browser on your device, and M5 never sends them to the publisher. When you sign in to a health system, the access it grants is read-only and is used only to download your records into your browser; the access token is discarded when the import finishes. The privacy policy describes every case where information leaves your browser, and each one happens only when you choose it.

Your responsibilities

  • Only import records you have the right to access: your own, or those of someone you are authorized to act for, such as your child through parent (proxy) access.
  • Keep your device secure. Anyone who can use your browser can see what you have imported. Use Clear in the app, or clear your browser's site data, to remove it.
  • Treat SMART Health Links and their QR codes like the records they open: anyone who has one can read what it shares.

Not medical, legal or financial advice

M5 shows what your records and public plan data say. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and its plan comparisons and cost estimates are not quotes or recommendations. Always talk with your doctor, pharmacist or insurer before making decisions about your care or coverage. In an emergency, call 911.

Accuracy

Records come from your health systems and other sources, and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. M5 can misread data it does not recognize. If something looks wrong, check it with the source.

Other services

M5 connects to services run by others, such as your health system, the PIQI Gateway, the CMS Marketplace API and the National Library of Medicine's RxNav. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you use them through M5.
